## Reading

### Range cap

The cap rule's agreement statistic is CENSORED by the pairing: `d_range` is
`|p_zed| - |p_lidar|` for a reference chosen within 0.6 m in 3D, so
`|d_range| <= 0.6` by construction and its MAD cannot reach the rule's 1.0 m
threshold whatever the data does. The only condition left able to fail is the
>= 200-pair floor.

That is what happened here: the rule returned 40 m, bound by `saturated_at_bin_loop_limit`, not by any
measured disagreement. The number is an artefact of the method and is NOT written to the
config; `stereo_range_cap_m` keeps its prior value.

The uncensored view is `frac_paired`, the share of a ring's points in the bin that
found any LiDAR reference at all:

- ring 100: peaks at 1.00 in the 1-2 m bin; first falls below 0.50 in the 10-11 m bin, 0.07 in the 39-40 m bin.
- ring 101: peaks at 1.00 in the 3-4 m bin; first falls below 0.50 in the 7-8 m bin, 0.02 in the 39-40 m bin.
